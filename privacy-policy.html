PiStats Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

PiStats is an Android app for monitoring a Raspberry Pi through a self-hosted backend. This Privacy Policy explains what PiStats stores, how it communicates, and what data is under your control.

Information PiStats stores

PiStats stores the following information locally on your Android device:

  • The backend base URL you configure
  • The backend authentication token you configure, encrypted using Android Keystore
  • Your media-backup preferences and upload checkpoint, when enabled
  • Cached widget state derived from backend responses

Information PiStats transmits

PiStats sends requests only to the backend address you configure. These requests may include:

  • Authenticated telemetry requests to read Raspberry Pi status
  • Authenticated Wake-on-LAN requests when you use the Wake PC action
  • Photos and videos you authorize Android to make available, only when you enable media backup

Personal data

PiStats does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal data such as your name, email address, contacts, messages, or location. If you enable media backup, the app processes the photos and videos you authorize solely to upload them to the self-hosted backend you configured.

Analytics and advertising

PiStats does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use third-party analytics services.

Data sharing

PiStats does not sell your data and does not share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Any data visible in the app comes from the backend you operate or choose to connect to.

Backend responsibility

PiStats is designed to work with a self-hosted backend. Data handling on that backend, including logs, access controls, backups, or network exposure, is controlled by the backend operator.

Security

PiStats is intended to be used with a private backend over a trusted LAN, Tailscale, or an HTTPS endpoint. The app restricts cleartext HTTP configuration to private-network addresses. You are responsible for protecting the backend token and keeping the service private.

Children

PiStats is not directed to children and is intended as a technical utility application.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published at the same URL where this policy is hosted.

Contact

If you need to contact the developer about this policy, use the support or contact details provided in the app listing or repository profile.
