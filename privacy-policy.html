PiStats Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

PiStats is an Android app for monitoring a Raspberry Pi through a self-hosted backend. This Privacy Policy explains what PiStats stores, how it communicates, and what data is under your control.

Information PiStats stores

PiStats stores the following information locally on your Android device:

  • The backend base URL you configure
  • The backend authentication token you configure, encrypted using Android Keystore
  • Your media-backup preferences and upload checkpoint, when enabled
  • Normalized transaction details, review corrections, and safe sync diagnostics in an Android-Keystore-encrypted queue, when transaction sync is enabled
  • Cached widget state derived from backend responses

Information PiStats transmits

PiStats sends requests only to the backend address you configure. These requests may include:

  • Authenticated telemetry requests to read Raspberry Pi status
  • Authenticated Wake-on-LAN requests when you use the Wake PC action
  • Photos and videos you authorize Android to make available, only when you enable media backup
  • Normalized transaction fields that you review and approve, only when you explicitly sync them

SMS transaction processing

If you explicitly enable transaction capture and grant SMS permission, PiStats processes future incoming SMS messages on your device to identify financial transaction alerts. It does not read existing inbox history. OTP, failed, pending, promotional, and balance-only messages are ignored. The complete SMS body is not stored and is never sent to the PiStats backend, Firebase, or Actual Budget. Only a normalized transaction that you review and approve can be sent to your configured self-hosted backend.

Personal data

PiStats does not require a PiStats account and does not intentionally collect your name, contacts, or location. If you enable media backup, the app processes the photos and videos you authorize solely to upload them to the self-hosted backend you configured. If you enable transaction capture, the app processes future financial SMS alerts as described above.

Analytics and advertising

PiStats does not include advertising SDKs. It uses Google Firebase Analytics for aggregate app-usage measurement and Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics. Depending on the platform and Firebase configuration, these services may process app interactions, crash traces, app and device metadata, and identifiers used for analytics or diagnostics. PiStats does not intentionally send backend tokens, backend passwords, SMS bodies, normalized transaction fields, or user media to Firebase. See Firebase Privacy and Security for information about Google's processing.

Data sharing

PiStats does not sell your data or share it for advertising. Google processes analytics and crash-diagnostic data as a service provider. Feature data visible in the app otherwise comes from, or is sent to, the backend you operate or choose to connect to.

Backend responsibility

PiStats is designed to work with a self-hosted backend. Data handling on that backend, including logs, access controls, backups, or network exposure, is controlled by the backend operator.

Security

PiStats is intended to be used with a private backend over a trusted LAN, Tailscale, or an HTTPS endpoint. The app restricts cleartext HTTP configuration to private-network addresses. You are responsible for protecting the backend token and keeping the service private.

Children

PiStats is not directed to children and is intended as a technical utility application.
